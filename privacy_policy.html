Privacy Policy

App: Dice Roller  |  Developer: Innovexa Technologies  |  Effective Date: August 30, 2026

1. Data Collection & Usage

Dice Roller does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The application functions entirely offline and does not require account creation, login, or internet access.

2. Permissions

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile vibration feedback when rolling dice. It has no access to private files, camera, microphone, or contacts.

3. Local Storage

User preferences (such as dice themes, sounds, and vibration settings) and roll history are stored strictly on the local device. This data never leaves the device and can be cleared at any time within the app.

4. Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13, in full compliance with COPPA and GDPR.
