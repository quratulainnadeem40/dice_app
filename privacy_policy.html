Official Privacy Policy

Dice Roller Application

Innovexa Technologies  |  Effective Date: August 30, 2026

Privacy Commitment: The Dice Roller app created by Innovexa Technologies is designed with privacy-by-default. We DO NOT collect, store, share, sell, or transmit any personal information or device tracking data. The application operates 100% locally on your device.

1. Information Collection & Usage

We respect your privacy. Our application does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.

  • No Account Required: You can use all features of the app without registration or sign-in.
  • No Tracking: We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or behavioral trackers.
  • 100% Offline: All dice rolling simulations and voice announcers operate locally on your device.

2. System Permissions Requested

The app only requests the minimum essential system permission required for tactile feedback:

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used strictly to provide physical tactile vibration when rolling dice. This permission has zero access to private files, camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

3. Local Data Storage & User Control

The app saves non-personal preferences locally on your device for user experience continuity:

  • App Settings: Chosen dice themes, custom colors, animation speed, sound volume, and vibration intensity.
  • Roll History: Past roll records, player names, and winner highlights.

All stored data remains strictly inside your local device storage and is never uploaded to any cloud server. You can erase all saved history at any time using the "Clear History" button inside the app.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

This application contains no third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or external monetization services.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Our application is family-friendly and suitable for users of all ages (3+). Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, we fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global data protection regulations.

6. Security of Your Data

Since no personal information is transmitted across the internet or stored on external servers, your data is completely secure from unauthorized access, interception, or server breaches.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Effective Date".
