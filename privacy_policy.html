Privacy Policy

Dice Roller Application

Innovex Technologies | Last Updated: August 30, 2026

Privacy Summary: The Dice Roller app by Innovex Technologies is designed with complete user privacy in mind. We DO NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data or usage analytics. The application operates 100% locally on your device.

1. Information Collection & Usage

We respect your privacy. Our application does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.

  • No Account Required: You can use all features of the app without signing up or creating an account.
  • No Tracking: We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, or behavioral trackers.
  • No Internet Needed: The core features of this application operate completely offline.

2. Permissions Requested

The app only requests the minimum essential system permissions required for core dice rolling functionality:

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used strictly to provide tactile haptic feedback when dice are rolled or interacted with. This permission cannot access private data, files, camera, microphone, or contacts.

3. Local Data Storage

The app saves certain non-personal preferences locally on your device for user experience continuity:

  • App Settings: Chosen dice themes, colors, sound volume, animation speed, and vibration intensity.
  • Game History: Past roll records, player names, and winner highlights.

All stored data remains strictly on your local device and is never uploaded to any cloud server. You can erase all saved history at any time using the "Clear History" button inside the app.

4. Third-Party Services & Libraries

This application is built using Flutter and open-source UI libraries. We do not integrate third-party advertising networks (e.g., AdMob), analytics services (e.g., Firebase Analytics), or user profiling platforms.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is family-friendly and suitable for users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, we fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and general data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA).

6. Security of Your Data

Since no personal information is transmitted across the internet or stored on external servers, your data cannot be exposed to unauthorized access, interception, or server breaches.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
