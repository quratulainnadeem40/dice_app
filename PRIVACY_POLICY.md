## 1. Overview
Innovex Technologies built the **Dice Roller** app as a free, offline-first utility application. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

We **DO NOT** collect, store, share, or sell any personal data or usage analytics. The app functions 100% locally on your device.

---

## 2. Information Collection & Use
We respect your privacy:
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details.
- **No Analytics / Tracking:** We do not track your activity, IP address, device ID, or usage habits.
- **Offline Nature:** The app does not require an active internet connection to roll dice, configure settings, or view history.

---

## 3. Device Permissions
The application requests only the minimum required permission:
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Required solely to trigger tactile haptic vibration when rolling dice. This permission has zero access to photos, files, contacts, camera, or microphone.

---

## 4. Local Data Storage
The app stores non-personal preferences locally on your device (using local app sandbox storage):
- Selected dice theme & color
- Sound volume & vibration intensity preferences
- Roll history records and custom player names

This data is stored purely on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. You can clear this data at any time from the in-app "Clear History" option or by clearing the app data.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services
The app does **not** include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or external monetization services.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)
Our app is safe and family-friendly for all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13.
