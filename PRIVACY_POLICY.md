**Developer:** Innovexa Technologies

---

### 1. Data Collection & Privacy
Dice Roller does **not collect, store, or share any personal data**. The application operates 100% locally and offline without requiring user registration, sign-in, or tracking.

---

### 2. Device Permissions
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Strictly used to trigger physical haptic feedback when dice are rolled. It cannot access photos, files, camera, mic, or personal data.

---

### 3. Local Data Storage
User settings (themes, sound volume, vibration intensity) and roll history records are stored purely inside the device's local sandbox storage. This data is never sent to external servers and can be wiped anytime via the in-app "Clear History" button.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
The app contains **no advertising SDKs, no behavioral trackers, and no external analytics**.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)
The app is safe for all ages and does not collect any data from children or any users.
